# **=== Vidfast Usage ===**
Url: http://vidfast.netlify.app/

## **MOVIE**
1. Open https://www.themoviedb.org/.
1. Search for and find the movie you want.
1. Click it, and make sure your URL looks something like this: https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/xxx-movie-name
1. Take the 'www.themoviedb.org' part and change it to 'vidfast.netlify.app', and press enter.
1. On the website, use the dropdown menu to choose a mirror, or turn on auto-redirect in the top right.
1. Voila! Movie streaming...

### **E.G.**
*. I chose the movie Spider-Man.
*. This is the URL: https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/557-spider-man.
*. After changing, the URL looks like this: https://vidfast.netlify.app/movie/557-spider-man.

## **SERIES**
1. Open https://www.themoviedb.org/.
1. Search and find the series you want.
1. Click it, and make sure your URL looks something like this: https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/xxx-name-of-series
1. If you want a specific season or episode, locate it on the website. Your new URL should look something like this: https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/xxx-name-of-series/season/x/episode/x.
1. Take the 'www.themoviedb.org' part and change it to 'vidfast.netlify.app', and press enter.
1. On the website, use the dropdown menu to choose a mirror, or turn on auto-redirect in the top right.
1. Voila! Series streaming...

### **E.G.**
*. I chose the series, Tom and Jerry.
*. This is the URL: https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/47480-the-tom-and-jerry-show.
*. This is the URL after I located my specific episode: https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/47480-the-tom-and-jerry-show/season/3/episode/2.
*. After changing, the URL looks like this: https://vidfast.netlify.app/tv/47480-the-tom-and-jerry-show/season/3/episode/2.

## **WARNING**
Make sure you have a good ad-blocker installed. I recommend uBlock Origin Lite. Set your ad-blocker's filtering mode to the highest level in its settings.

The website that streams movies or series is a pirate website. You won't get in trouble, but some mirrors may go down at some points, so use other mirrors.
